How the call is made

Price position. NQ above the prior period's high scores +2, above its midpoint +1, below its midpoint −1, below its low −2. The day call uses the prior day, the week call the prior week, the month call the prior month.

Data surprises. Each release that beats or misses its forecast adds points to the day and week calls. While the Fed is fighting inflation, strong numbers point to higher rates, which hurt tech. So hotter inflation, stronger growth, spending and housing count as bearish, and higher jobless claims count as bullish. Weights: core PCE MoM 3, headline PCE MoM 2, core PCE YoY 2, spending 1, GDP 1, GDP price index 1, everything else 0.25–0.5. Releases matching their forecast score 0.

The call. A total of +1 or more is bullish, −1 or less is bearish, anything in between is neutral (no clear edge).

This is a rules-based bias for information only, not trading or investment advice. Forecasts: Forex Factory. Actuals: FRED (St. Louis Fed), usually within minutes of release. NQ price: Yahoo Finance, may be delayed. Refreshes every 60 seconds.
